Agreement & Activation

Anyone can mail every mailbox and hope. Roughly 1,945 households across Rutherford and Polk counties heat with oil or kerosene — about one home in eighteen. We know which ones, we verify them, and this program puts the Lutz name in front of exactly those households — two cards, every month: a referral card to the customers you already serve, and a new-business card to the oil-heat homes you don't. Yet.

What's live the day you sign

  • The verified oil-heat home audience — the ~1,945-household universe across your seventeen ZIP codes, resolved to specific, verified addresses: built from housing & property records plus live heating-research behavior, challenged and cross-validated before a single piece mails.
  • Your current customers, sorted to the right message — your ~600 accounts almost certainly sit inside that 1,945-home universe. Once your customer list is separated, they move to the referral track — and the new-business card goes only to the homes you don't already serve. No wasted postage, no wrong message in the wrong mailbox.
  • Two cards, two jobs — a new-business postcard to the verified oil-heat homes, and a referral card to your current customers with its own offer. Different message, different code, both measured.
  • Design included — we design both cards with you: the heritage photo, the family, the trucks, the offer — and a QR code that can carry a personal video message from the owner.
  • A real scoreboard — dedicated tracking number and offer code on every piece, reported against the true ~1,945-home denominator. You'll know what every drop produced.
  • Season timing — first drop targeted for the end of September, follow-ups two to three weeks apart as temperatures fall. You approve every deployment before it mails.
